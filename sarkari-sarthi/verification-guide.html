How to verify a government job notification in India

Recruitment details can change through corrigenda, deadline extensions, revised vacancy tables or updated examination instructions. A job listing is a useful starting point, but the original recruiting authority's notice is the final reference. This guide explains how to check a vacancy before sharing personal information or submitting an application.

1. Identify the recruiting authority

Start with the name of the department, commission, board, university, public-sector organisation or institution conducting the recruitment. Search for the same advertisement number on that organisation's official website. Do not assume that a familiar logo or a copied notice makes a third-party page official.

2. Open the original notification

Prefer a PDF or notice hosted on the recruiting authority's own website. Check that the post title, advertisement number, publication date and organisation match the listing. Read any corrigendum or addendum published after the original notice; a later official update may change eligibility, vacancies, fees or dates.

3. Compare the important details

  • Vacancies: Check the total posts and any category-wise or post-wise breakdown.
  • Eligibility: Confirm the required degree, subject, experience, registration or other conditions.
  • Age: Read the reference date, minimum and maximum age, and applicable relaxation rules.
  • Dates: Verify application start and closing dates, fee deadlines, exam dates and correction windows.
  • Fees: Confirm the amount, payment method, exemptions and refund rules from the official notice.
  • Selection: Check the stages, examination pattern, interview, skill test or document verification requirements.

4. Understand Sarkari Sarthi verification labels

Official source

The record is backed by an official source or a captured official notification/application link. Still open the original notice because the authority may publish a later correction.

Official link pending

An official domain has been identified, but a direct notification or application link has not yet been confirmed. Treat the record as a lead, not as final application guidance.

Secondary source

The vacancy was discovered through a non-government employment portal. Use it to find the possible recruitment, then independently locate the notice on the recruiting authority's website.

Needs review

The record may be stale, incomplete or have a link requiring another check. Do not rely on missing or uncertain details; use the original authority notice.

5. Apply safely

  1. Type or navigate to the recruiting authority's official website yourself where possible.
  2. Check the domain carefully before entering a phone number, identity details or payment information.
  3. Never pay a person or unrelated website promising selection, a guaranteed appointment or special access.
  4. Save the notification PDF, application number, submitted form and payment receipt.

6. What Sarkari Sarthi does—and does not do

Sarkari Sarthi is an independent job-information portal operated by GoGo Company. It collects recruitment leads from public employment sources, organizes them for search and displays source and verification labels. It is not a government agency, recruiting authority or application-processing service. A listing does not guarantee that applications are open or that every field has been independently confirmed.

We do not invent vacancy counts, salaries, eligibility conditions or dates when the source data does not provide them. Missing information should be checked in the official notification. Expired or cancelled records are marked accordingly and should not be treated as open vacancies.

7. Request a correction

If you find an incorrect deadline, broken official link, duplicate listing or changed recruitment notice, contact GoGo Company with the job title or job ID and a link to the relevant official source. Include the advertisement number where available so the record can be identified accurately.
