How to verify a government job notification in India

Recruitment details can change through corrigenda, deadline extensions, revised vacancy tables or updated examination instructions. A job listing is a starting point—not the final authority. Before sharing personal information, paying an application fee or submitting a form, confirm the notice on the recruiting organisation's own website.

1. Official recruitment and examination portals

Use these official portals as starting points. The list is selected, not exhaustive. Some recruitments are handled by a regional board or a dedicated application subdomain, so follow the notice to the exact authority named in the advertisement.

  • National Career Service (NCS): Government of India's employment and career-services portal. Check the employer and recruiting authority details shown for each opportunity.
  • Union Public Service Commission (UPSC): Civil Services and other Union recruitment advertisements, examination notices and official updates.
  • Staff Selection Commission (SSC): Central recruitment notices, examination calendars, candidate notices and application services.
  • Railway Recruitment Board, Chandigarh: Railway employment notices and candidate updates. Always check which RRB is named in the specific Centralised Employment Notice.
  • Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB): Madhya Pradesh state-level selection examinations, recruitment advertisements and candidate updates.
  • Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission (MPPSC): State civil-service and other MPPSC recruitment advertisements, exam notices and results.
  • Employment News: Government of India's weekly employment publication and vacancy notices. Open the original recruiting organisation's notice before applying.
  • Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS): Banking recruitment and examination notifications administered by IBPS.
  • National Testing Agency (NTA): Official examination notices, schedules and candidate information for NTA-administered tests. It is an examination authority, not a general jobs portal.
  • Reserve Bank of India — Opportunities: RBI recruitment advertisements, application information and related notices.
  • State Bank of India — Current Openings: SBI recruitment advertisements and official application information.
  • India Post GDS Online Engagement: Gramin Dak Sevak engagement notifications, registration and application updates.
  • Join Indian Army: Indian Army recruitment notices, eligibility information and candidate services.

Portal addresses and recruitment processes can change. If a link is unavailable, start from the organisation's main official website and navigate to its recruitment, careers, advertisement or candidate-notice section. Do not rely on a copied URL alone.

2. Match the notification to the recruiting authority

Find the department, commission, board, university, public-sector organisation or institution responsible for the vacancy. Search for the same advertisement number or notification title on that authority's website. A familiar logo, copied PDF or HTTPS lock does not make a third-party page an official source.

  • Advertisement number: Match the number, post title, department and publication date.
  • Original notice: Prefer the notification PDF or notice hosted by the recruiting authority. Check later corrigenda, addenda and cancellation notices.
  • Vacancies: Compare total posts and any category-wise, region-wise or post-wise breakdown.
  • Eligibility: Confirm education, subject, experience, registration and other conditions exactly as written.
  • Age: Check the age reference date, minimum and maximum limits, and applicable relaxation rules.
  • Dates: Verify application start and closing dates, fee deadlines, correction windows, examination dates and document-verification schedules.
  • Fees: Confirm the amount, exemptions, payment method and refund rules from the original notice.
  • Selection: Read the examination stages, skill test, interview, physical test and document-verification requirements.

3. Check the domain and application link

Many Indian government portals use .gov.in or .nic.in. Some recognised recruiting authorities use other official domains, including ibps.in, nta.ac.in, rbi.org.in, sbi.bank.in and bank.sbi. These examples are not a blanket approval for every similar-looking domain; confirm the exact host from the authority's own website.

  • Read the domain from right to left and check the exact spelling. A lookalike such as ssc-gov.example is not ssc.gov.in.
  • An HTTPS padlock only indicates an encrypted connection; it does not prove that the organisation is genuine.
  • Be cautious of shortened URLs, personal payment accounts, unofficial messaging-only application links or anyone promising selection for money.
  • Where an official notice links to a separate application host, follow that link from the official notice rather than trusting a message or advertisement.

4. Understand Sarkari Sarthi's source labels

Sarkari Sarthi displays source and verification labels so readers can judge how much evidence is available. A label is not a guarantee that a vacancy is still open; always check the latest notice and corrigendum.

Official Source

The record is backed by a recognised official recruiting-authority source or an official notification/application link. Confirm that no later notice has changed the details.

Official Link Pending

An official domain has been identified, but a direct notification or application link has not yet been captured. Treat the listing as a lead and locate the original notice yourself.

Secondary Source

The vacancy was discovered through a non-government employment portal. Use it to identify a possible recruitment, then independently verify the advertisement on the recruiting authority's website.

Needs Review

The record may be incomplete, stale or have conflicting information. Do not rely on missing or uncertain details; check the original authority notice.

Expired or cancelled vacancies should not be treated as open applications. If a record has no verification label, do not assume it is official.

5. Apply safely and keep proof

  1. Navigate to the recruiting authority's official site and open the exact notification.
  2. Check the application host and fee instructions against the notice before entering personal details or making a payment.
  3. Never pay an individual or unrelated website for a guaranteed job, appointment letter, interview or special access.
  4. Save the original notification, submitted form, application number, payment receipt and confirmation page.
  5. Check the authority's website again before the deadline in case a corrigendum or extension has been published.

6. What Sarkari Sarthi does—and does not do

A listing does not guarantee that applications are open or that an application will be accepted.

7. Report a correction

If you find a changed deadline, broken official link, duplicate listing or later recruitment notice, contact GoGo Company with the job title or job ID, advertisement number and the original authority URL. This helps us identify the exact record and review the correction.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sarkari Sarthi a government website?

No. Sarkari Sarthi is an independent information portal operated by GoGo Company. Applications and final recruitment decisions are handled by the named recruiting authority.

Are all vacancies on Sarkari Sarthi officially verified?

No. Each available source label describes the evidence attached to that record. A Secondary Source or Needs Review label means you should verify the notice independently before applying.

Where should I apply for a job?

Apply only through the application route stated by the recruiting authority in its original notification. Sarkari Sarthi does not accept applications or fees.
