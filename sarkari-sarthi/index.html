How to verify a government job listing

Job information can first appear on an employment portal, a department website or an official recruitment notice. Sarkari Sarthi shows the available source and a verification label so readers can judge what has—and has not—been confirmed. A listing on this portal is never a substitute for the original recruitment advertisement.

Official source

The record has an official government source or a captured official notification/application link. Open that link and compare the post, dates and eligibility before applying.

Official link pending

An official domain has been identified, but a direct notification or application link still needs confirmation. Do not treat the secondary listing as final evidence.

Secondary source

The information was discovered through a non-government employment source. Use it only as a discovery lead and locate the recruiting authority's original notice.

Needs review

The record is incomplete, stale or has a link that needs checking. Avoid relying on it until the official notice confirms the details.

Before you submit an application

  1. Confirm the recruiting organisation, advertisement number and post title in the official notice.
  2. Check the application start date, last date, exam date and any corrigendum or extension.
  3. Compare qualification, age limit, category rules, vacancies, salary and application fee with the notice.
  4. Apply only through the official recruiting authority's website. Do not pay fees through an unrelated link.
  5. Save the official notification, application confirmation and payment receipt for your records.

When a date, eligibility detail or source link appears incorrect, please report the job ID and supporting official source through the GoGo Company contact page. We review correction requests and update or remove records when the available evidence supports a change.
