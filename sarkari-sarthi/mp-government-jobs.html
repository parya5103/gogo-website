Madhya Pradesh government jobs: official recruitment portals

Use this directory to reach the recruiting authority before trusting a job post, application link or social-media message. It is a guide to official sources—not a live vacancy list. A portal appearing here does not mean that recruitment is currently open.

Madhya Pradesh recruitment authorities

  • Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB): Recruitment tests and selection processes conducted by MPESB. Check the student dashboard for exam-wise rulebooks, application windows, admit cards, answer keys and results.
  • Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission (MPPSC): State public-service examinations, recruitment advertisements, examination notices and results. Read the latest advertisement and every subsequent corrigendum.
  • High Court of Madhya Pradesh — Recruitment & Results: Notices, advertisements, examination updates, results and application-status information for High Court and district-court recruitments.

Official application and employment-service portals

  • MPOnline: Madhya Pradesh's citizen-services and application-facilitation portal. It may host application forms for a recruiting department, but the department named in the advertisement remains the authority for eligibility, vacancies, dates and corrections.
  • National Career Service (NCS): Government of India's employment-services portal. Use its government job vacancies directory as a starting point, then confirm the notice on the recruiting organisation's own website. NCS warns users about fraudulent parties misusing its name.
  • Employment News — All Jobs: Government of India's employment publication. Check the issue/advertisement date and last date carefully; an old listing is not evidence that applications are still open.

Central recruitment portals relevant to MP applicants

  • Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) — central civil services and other Union recruitment.
  • Staff Selection Commission (SSC) — central government recruitment examinations.
  • Railway Recruitment Board, Chandigarh — one regional RRB portal; always use the RRB named in the relevant Centralised Employment Notice.
  • Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) — banking recruitment examinations administered by IBPS.

How to verify a vacancy before applying

  1. Open the recruiting authority's original advertisement or notification PDF—not just a summary on a third-party website.
  2. Match the post name, advertisement number, category-wise vacancies, qualification, age limits and fee with the original notice.
  3. Check the application start date and last date. Look for later corrigenda, extensions, cancellations and revised rulebooks.
  4. Use only the application URL named by the authority. An application-facilitation portal is not automatically the recruiting authority.
  5. Pay a fee only when the official notification specifies it and only through the authorised payment route. Never pay an agent to obtain a government job.

How Sarkari Sarthi labels its listings

Our source labels describe the evidence available for an individual record. “Official source” means the record is backed by a recognised official source; “Secondary source” and “Needs review” mean the original notice still needs independent checking. A label is not a guarantee that a vacancy is open today.
