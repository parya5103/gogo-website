How to enter cheat codes in GTA 6

  1. Finish Jason and Lucia's main story, according to the specialist guides.
  2. Complete the individual challenge required to unlock the cheat.
  3. Open the in-game phone with D-pad Up while in free roam.
  4. On the phone's main screen, hold L1 + R1 on PlayStation or LB + RB on Xbox, then enter the listed D-pad sequence.
  5. Check the Progress menu's Cheats list if an input does not work.

Warning: reports say activating cheats blocks saving and disables trophies/achievements for that play session. Do not use cheats during a session where you need to save progress or earn achievements.

GTA 6 frequently asked questions

What is the GTA 6 release date?

Rockstar's official GTA VI page lists 19 November 2026.

Which platforms support GTA 6?

Rockstar currently lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. No PC release date is announced on the official platform listing checked for this guide.

Are GTA 6 cheat codes officially confirmed by Rockstar?

Rockstar has not published an official cheat-code list. Specialist outlets have reported cheat inputs and unlock requirements. This guide labels them as reported rather than Rockstar-confirmed.

How many GTA 6 cheats are there?

PowerPyx and GamesRadar+ list 61 entries across player, combat, vehicle, environment and screen-filter categories. The number of publicly printed button sequences is lower because many remain TBC.

Is there a GTA 6 money cheat?

PowerPyx and GamesRadar+ report a $250,000 cheat with the sequence Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right. It is reported to unlock after completing the story. GoGo Company has not independently tested it.

Do GTA 5 cheat codes work in GTA 6?

Do not assume that GTA V inputs work in GTA VI. Use only inputs specifically reported for GTA VI and check their unlock requirements.

Can I save after using GTA 6 cheats?

Launch-day guides report that saving is disabled while a cheat is active and that trophies/achievements are disabled for the play session. Verify the behaviour in your current game version before using a cheat.

Can I play GTA 6 on PC?

The Rockstar platform information checked on 19 November 2026 lists PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. It does not list a PC version or PC release date.

Is GTA 6 Online available?

This guide does not claim an online launch date or feature set. Check Rockstar Newswire for official multiplayer announcements.
