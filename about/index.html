About us

We build small things, properly

GoGo Company is an independent gaming and digital products company. We ship free browser games and practical web tools, and we keep them fast, honest and free.

What we do

We make browser-based games that run on any device without downloads, and information products that respect your time. Our current lineup:

  • Arrow Out! - a minimalist hexagon-arrow puzzle game.
  • Crystal Quest - a story-driven match-3 adventure with offline PWA support.
  • Sarkari Sarthi - a government job notification tracker sourced from official websites.

Our principles

  • Free to use. Our games have no paywalls and our tools have no charges.
  • Mobile-first. Everything we build works well on a phone browser first.
  • Honest information. Sarkari listings always link official government sources. We never invent vacancies, dates or eligibility.
  • Small and maintainable. Lightweight pages, no heavy frameworks, fast loads on slow connections.
