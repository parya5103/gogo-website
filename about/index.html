About us

We build small things, properly

GoGo Company is an independent gaming and digital products company. We ship free browser games and practical web tools, and we keep them fast, honest and free.

What we do

We make browser-based games that run on any device without downloads, and information products that respect your time. Our current lineup:

  • Arrow Out! - a minimalist hexagon-arrow puzzle game.
  • Crystal Quest - a story-driven match-3 adventure with offline PWA support.
  • Sarkari Sarthi - a government job notification tracker sourced from official websites.

Our principles

  • Free to use. Our games have no paywalls and our tools have no charges.
  • Mobile-first. Everything we build works well on a phone browser first.
  • Honest information. Sarkari listings always link official government sources. We never invent vacancies, dates or eligibility.
  • Small and maintainable. Lightweight pages, no heavy frameworks, fast loads on slow connections.

Sarkari Sarthi and job information

Sarkari Sarthi is our government-job information portal. It gathers publicly available recruitment notifications - job openings, admit cards, results and exam updates - from available recruitment sources such as the official Employme nt News publication and public job portals, and organises them in one place.

  • Verify before you act: listings are for convenience only. Always confirm dates, eligibility, vacancies and fees against the official recruitment notification before applying.
  • Official links only: application links point to the relevant recruiting authority or official source, so every step happens on the official portal.
  • Not a government body: GoGo Company does not represent any government department or recruiting authority and is not affiliated with one.
  • No guarantees: we do not guarantee recruitment, selection or employment. Outcomes depend entirely on the official process of the recruiting authority.
  • Our aim: accessible, clear and useful information - plain summaries, honest gaps, no invented details.
