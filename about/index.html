About us

About Gogo Company

Gogo Company (gogocompany.in) is an independent Indian technology, digital media and web gaming organization headquartered in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India, founded by Pratik Arya.

What we do

We build browser-based games, career-information tools, news discovery and AI research resources. Our current product lineup:

  • Arrow Out! - a minimalist hexagon-arrow puzzle game with 1000 levels, hints and daily challenges.
  • Crystal Quest: Gem Legends - a story-driven match-3 adventure with 27 levels across 3 regions and offline PWA support.
  • Sarkari Sarthi - a government recruitment information portal with source links and verification context.
  • PikaNews - an automatic, publisher-attributed news discovery feed refreshed every 30 minutes.
  • GoGo AI Intelligence - a source-linked hub for global AI news, model discovery, research and provider-declared comparisons.

Our principles

  • Free to use. Our games have no paywalls and our tools have no charges.
  • Mobile-first. Everything we build works well on a phone browser first.
  • Honest information. Sarkari Sarthi labels source and verification status; secondary reports are not presented as official confirmation. We never invent vacancies, dates or eligibility.
  • Small and maintainable. Lightweight pages, no heavy frameworks, fast loads on slow connections.

Sarkari Sarthi and job information

Sarkari Sarthi is our government-recruitment information portal. It organizes notices from a mix of official recruiting-authority publications and secondary public sources. Each listing should be checked against the original authority notice; a secondary-source listing is not an official confirmation.

  • Verify before you act: listings are for convenience only. Always confirm dates, eligibility, vacancies and fees against the official recruitment notification before applying.
  • Verify before applying: use the source and verification labels to understand the listing's evidence, then navigate to the recruiting authority's own notice and application portal before sharing information or paying any fee.
  • Not a government body: GoGo Company does not represent any government department or recruiting authority and is not affiliated with one.
  • No guarantees: we do not guarantee recruitment, selection or employment. Outcomes depend entirely on the official process of the recruiting authority.
  • Our aim: accessible, clear and useful information - plain summaries, honest gaps, no invented details.
