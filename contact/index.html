What to include

  • Which product (Arrow Out!, Crystal Quest, Sarkari Sarthi)
  • Your device and browser
  • What happened, and what you expected
  • For listing errors: the listing title and the correct official source

Product support

For Arrow Out! or Crystal Quest, include your device model, browser version and the steps that led to the issue. Never email passwords, verification codes or private account data.

For Sarkari Sarthi corrections, include the vacancy title and a link to the official recruitment notification. For PikaNews corrections, include the article URL and the original publisher source.

Before you contact support

For a game issue, first check that your browser is up to date, JavaScript is enabled and the page has finished loading. If the problem continues, describe the steps that reproduce it. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, payment details or other sensitive information in your message.

For a job-listing correction, use the recruitment organisation’s official notification as the reference. Include the official notice URL and identify the exact field that needs review, such as the application deadline, eligibility or vacancy details. We can review the information but cannot submit applications or make recruitment decisions.

For a PikaNews correction, share the article URL and the original publisher’s source so the editorial information can be checked. Please distinguish a factual correction from a general opinion or suggestion.
