Arrow Out!

A minimalist puzzle of winding arrow paths. Tap a piece to launch it out of the board - but only if its exit corridor is clear. Easy to learn, genuinely tricky to master.

Play Now (opens in a new tab) Try Crystal Quest

How to play

  • Tap a piece to launch it along its arrow path
  • A piece launches only if its head's exit corridor is clear
  • Clear every arrow on the board to win

Features

  • 1000 campaign levels from 12x12 up to 65x65 boards
  • 3 free hints per level, hearts, stars and achievements
  • Daily challenges with streaks, skins shop, offline PWA
