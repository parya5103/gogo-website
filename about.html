About Gogo Company

Gogo Company (gogocompany.in) is an independent Indian technology, digital media and web gaming organization headquartered in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India. Founded by Pratik Arya, Gogo Company develops browser games and operates digital information and media portals.

Company overview

Gogo Company is the organization and digital brand represented by https://gogocompany.in/. Its work brings together browser-based entertainment, practical career information and publisher-attributed digital news. Each product has a dedicated page and a distinct purpose.

Founder

Pratik Arya is the founder of Gogo Company. The organization is based in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India.

Official products and portals

Sarkari Sarthi

Government recruitment alerts, exam notifications and career information with source references. Sarkari Sarthi is an independent information portal, not a government agency. Applicants should verify every notice and application detail with the original recruiting authority.

PikaNews

A digital news aggregation platform that presents publisher-attributed headlines and links readers to original reporting.

Arrow Out!

An HTML5 arrow-path logic puzzle game designed for play in a web browser.

Crystal Quest

An interactive HTML5 match-three fantasy puzzle adventure.
