Scope of this policy

This policy covers gogocompany.in - the GoGo Company website, the legal and information pages, and the product applications published under this domain:

  • Crystal Quest - gogocompany.in/crystalquest/

The games may display their own in-game privacy information. Where a game says something specific about its own features, that description also applies on this domain. We do not make privacy claims on behalf of any third-party site we link to.

Optional GoGo Account & Cloud Save

  • Guest play by default: You never need an account to use gogocompany.in, play Arrow Out! or Crystal Quest, or browse Sarkari Sarthi.
  • Optional GoGo Account: If you choose to create a GoGo Account (via Email/Password or Google Sign-In powered by Google Firebase Authentication), we store only essential account information in Google Cloud Firestore (users/{uid}): your email address, email verification status, display name (if provided), avatar URL (if provided by Google), authentication provider, marketing subscription status, registration timestamp, and last login timestamp.
  • Email verification: Password-based registrations receive a verification email via Firebase Authe ntication so you can confirm ownership of your email address.
  • Game Cloud Save (gameProfiles/{uid}/games/{gameId}): When signed in, your progress for Arrow Out! and Crystal Quest can be backed up and synced across devices in Cloud Firestore. If your local browser save and cloud save ever differ, you are prompted to choose between keeping local progress, restoring cloud progress, or smart-merging both.
  • Strict ownership & account deletion: Your cloud profile and game saves are accessible only to your authenticated account (and authorized GoGo administrators for account administration). You can permanently delete your GoGo Account and all associated Cloud Firestore data (users/{uid}, marketingSubscribers/{uid}, and gameProfiles/{uid}/games/*) at any time directly from the GoGo Account page or the in-game account modal.
  • We run no custom first-party analytics tracker of our own on this website.
  • The Sarkari Sarthi portal loads public job-data files in your browser and does not use GoGo Accounts.

Optional Promotional Emails & Marketing Subscribers

  • Separate, optional consent (unchecked by default): During email registration, you may optionally check the box: "Yes! I would like to receive GoGo Games news, new game announcements, updates, special offers and promotional emails." This checkbox is unchecked by default, and registration works completely without accepting marketing emails.
  • No automatic subscription on login or Google Sign-In: Signing in with Google or logging into your GoGo Account is never treated as marketing consent. Users who sign in with Google are never subscribed unless they explicitly opt in inside their account preferences.
    • < li>Verified emails only for promotional lists: Only users who have both explicitly opted in to marketing emails and verified their email address are eligible for promotional mailing list exports.
  • Consent accountability records: In our secure Cloud Firestore marketingSubscribers/{uid} collection, we record your email address, display name, email verification status, subscription status, consent timestamp, consent version/text, registration source, and unsubscribe timestamp for compliance and accountability. Normal users can never read, list, or export other users' email addresses.
  • Immediate unsubscribe: You can unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time with one click via the GoGo Unsubscribe Center (while signed in) or inside your GoGo Account settings, or by emailing officialgogocompany@gmail.com. Unsubscribe requests take effect immediately, preserve prior consent audit metadata as required for compliance, and we never re-add an unsubscribed user automatically.
  • No selling or third-party sharing: GoGo Company never sells, rents, or shares your email address or subscriber list with third parties.

What is stored in your browser

  • Game progress: Arrow Out! and Crystal Quest store your progress, settings and saves in your own browser's local storage under gogocompany.in. For guest players, this data remains in your browser on your device. Signed-in players who use Cloud Save also store a synchronized copy of their game save in their private GoGo Account in Cloud Firestore.
  • Authentication session: When you sign in to a GoGo Account, Firebase Authentication stores your session token locally in your browser (IndexedDB / local storage) so you stay signed in across gogocompany.in, /arrowout/, and /crystalquest/.
  • Offline files: The games install a service worker that caches game and account-interface files on your device so the games work offline. You can remove all local data at any time by signing out and clearing site data in your browser.

Cookies and similar technologies

  • For guest visitors, gogocompany.in sets no custom tracking cookies of its own. Signed-in GoGo Account sessions use browser IndexedDB / localStorage managed by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • When advertising units are switched on, Google AdSense / AdMob and their partners may set advertising cookies or use device identifiers - explained in the Advertising section below.
  • You can view, block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time.

What touches our systems and service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): When you register or sign in to a GoGo Account, Google Firebase processes your authentication credentials and stores your account profile, marketing consent record, and game cloud saves on our behalf.
  • Web server logs: Our static hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log standard technical request data such as IP address, browser type and requested pages, for security and abuse prevention, governed by GitHub's privacy policy.
  • Emails you send us: If you email us, we keep your message and address only to reply to you or process your support/privacy request.

How Google uses data from websites that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

For visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we only serve advertising after valid consent through a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP). Until a certified CMP is integrated and consent is obtained, advertising remains switched off for those regions - the ad areas simply do not load. Consent, where collected, covers personalised advertising, measurement and the cookies described above, and can be withdrawn at any time through the consent tool or your browser settings.

External links

Product pages, game links and Sarkari listings link to third-party and government websites. Once you leave gogocompany.in, their privacy policies apply, not ours.

Sarkari Sarthi listings in particular link to official recruitment websites and external job application portals run by recruiting authorities and other third parties. When you open those links or apply on those portals, any information you submit is handled under the privacy policy of that website - we never receive it.

Data security and retention

  • GoGo Account records, marketing consent records, and Cloud Save data are stored in Google Cloud Firestore protected by strict, UID-scoped Security Rules so that normal users can only access their own records.
  • Account data is retained for as long as you maintain your GoGo Account. When you use the "Delete Account" action in your GoGo Account settings, your Cloud Firestore profile, marketing subscriber record, and cloud game saves are deleted immediately along with your Firebase Authentication account.
  • Local browser game saves and offline cached files remain in your browser until you clear site data.
  • If you email us directly, we retain the email exchange only as long as needed to re solve your inquiry.

Children

Our website and games are family-friendly and are not directed at children under 13. Children under 13 should play in guest mode and must not create a GoGo Account or submit personal information. When advertising is active, ad personalisation for users known to be under 18 is restricted by Google's own advertising policies.
