Privacy Policy

Short version: this website collects almost nothing. No accounts, no analytics. When advertising is enabled, it is clearly labelled and works through Google AdSense - explained in full below.

Scope of this policy

This policy covers gogocompany.in - the GoGo Company website, the legal and information pages, and the product applications published under this domain:

  • Arrow Out! - gogocompany.in/arrowout/
  • Crystal Quest - gogocompany.in/crystalquest/
  • Sarkari Sarthi - gogocompany.in/sarkari-sarthi/

The games may display their own in-game privacy information. Where a game says something specific about its own features, that description also applies on this domain. We do not make privacy claims on behalf of any third-party site we link to.

Optional GoGo Account & Cloud Save

  • Guest play by default: You never need an account to use gogocompany.in, play Arrow Out! or Crystal Quest, or browse Sarkari Sarthi.
  • Optional GoGo Account: If you choose to create a GoGo Account (via Email/Password or Google Sign-In powered by Google Firebase Authentication), we collect only essential account information: your email address, display name (if provided), registration date, authentication provider, and marketing subscription status, alongside your game cloud save progress for Arrow Out! and Crystal Quest in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Strict ownership & deletion: Your cloud profile and game saves are accessible only to your authenticated account. You can delete your GoGo Account and all associated Cloud Firestore data at any time directly from the GoGo Account page or in-game account menu.
  • We run no third-party analytics or tracking scripts of our own on this website.
  • The Sarkari Sarthi portal loads public job-data files in your browser; it sends us no personal data.

Optional Promotional Emails & Marketing Subscribers

  • Separate, optional consent (unchecked by default): During email registration, you may optionally check the box: "Yes! I would like to receive GoGo Games news, new game announcements, updates, special offers and promotional emails." This checkbox is unchecked by default, and registration works completely without accepting marketing emails.
  • No automatic subscription on login or Google Sign-In: Signing in with Google or logging into your GoGo Account is never treated as marketing consent. Users who sign in with Google are not subscribed unless they explicitly opt in.
  • Consent accountability records: In our secure Cloud Firestore marketingSubscribers database, we record your subscription status, consent timestamp, consent version/text, and registration source for compliance and accountability. Normal users can never read or export other users' email addresses.
  • Immediate unsubscribe: You can unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time with one click via the GoGo Unsubscribe Center or inside your GoGo Account settings. Unsubscribe requests take effect immediately, and we never re-add an unsubscribed user automatically.
  • No selling or third-party sharing: GoGo Company never sells, rents, or shares your email address or subscriber list with third parties.

What is stored in your browser

  • Game progress: Arrow Out! and Crystal Quest store your progress, settings and saves in your own browser's local storage under gogocompany.in. For guest players, this data never leaves your device. Logged-in players who enable Cloud Backup also sync a copy of their game save to their private GoGo Account in Cloud Firestore.
  • Authentication session: When you sign in to a GoGo Account, Firebase Authentication stores your session token locally in your browser (IndexedDB / local storage) so you stay signed in across gogocompany.in, /arrowout/, and /crystalquest/.
  • Offline files: the games install a service worker that caches game files on your device so the games work offline. You can remove all of it any time by clearing site data in your browser.

Cookies and similar technologies

  • gogocompany.in sets no cookies of its own. There is no login, no analytics script and no preference cookie anywhere on this site.
  • The games keep progress in browser local storage on your device. Local storage is not a cookie and never leaves your device.
  • When advertising units are switched on, Google AdSense and its partners may set advertising cookies - explained in the next section.
  • You can view, block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. The site keeps working either way.

What may touch our systems

  • Web server logs: our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log standard technical request data such as IP address, browser type and requested pages, for security and abuse prevention. That data is handled under GitHub's own privacy policy.
  • Emails you send us: if you email us, we keep your message and address only to reply to you.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We may display advertising on informational pages of gogocompany.in (for example between content sections of the homepage and about page, and in one clearly separated area of the Sarkari Sarthi portal between its category grid and the job listings - never beside job cards or Apply buttons). Advertising is served through Google AdSense. Important points:

  • Clear labelling: every ad unit is visibly marked "Advertisement" and visually separated from content. Ads are never styled as, or mixed into, Sarkari job listings, and ads never appear inside the games or near game controls.
  • Third-party cookies: when ads are shown, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies (such as device identifiers and interaction data) to serve, measure and, where permitted, personalise ads, including the Google advertising cookie.
  • Personalised advertising: where allowed by law and consent rules, Google may use visit data to personalise the ads you see. Without that basis, ads are non-personalised.
  • Google's advertising and privacy information: how Google uses advertising cookies and similar technologies is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/ads, and the Google privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from websites that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Your choices: you can control personalised ads in Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com), review and manage your Google data at myadcenter.google.com, and opt out of many third-party vendor cookies at optout.aboutads.info. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser, which may affect how ads work.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

For visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we only serve advertising after valid consent through a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP). Until a certified CMP is integrated and consent is obtained, advertising remains switched off for those regions - the ad areas simply do not load. Consent, where collected, covers personalised advertising, measurement and the cookies described above, and can be withdrawn at any time through the consent tool or your browser settings.

External links

Product pages, game links and Sarkari listings link to third-party and government websites. Once you leave gogocompany.in, their privacy policies apply, not ours.

Sarkari Sarthi listings in particular link to official recruitment websites and external job application portals run by recruiting authorities and other third parties. When you open those links or apply on those portals, any information you submit is handled under the privacy policy of that website - we never receive it.

Data security and retention

  • This website is statically hosted. There is no visitor database, no comment system and no payment system on our side.
  • The only personal information we ever hold is the content of emails you send us. We keep an email exchange only as long as needed to handle your message.
  • Game saves and settings live only in your browser and are removed when you clear site data.
  • We cannot access data held by third parties such as Google or our hosting provider; the policies linked on this page explain your options with them.

Children

Our website and games are family-friendly and are not directed at children under 13. No part of gogocompany.in asks a child for personal information. When advertising is active, ad personalisation for users known to be under 18 is restricted by Google's own advertising policies.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page with a new effective date.

Your rights

  • You can ask us what email correspondence of yours we hold, request a correction, or ask us to delete the exchange - write to the address below and we will act on it.
  • Everything stored in your browser is under your control: clearing site data removes all game saves, offline files and any advertising cookies in one step.
  • You can change your Google ad personalisation settings at myadcenter.google.com and use the ad-industry choice tools listed above at any time.
  • Because we run no accounts and no analytics, there is no visitor profile on our side to access, export or delete.
