Privacy Policy

Short version: this website itself collects almost nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no ad networks on gogocompany.in.

Scope of this policy

This policy covers gogocompany.in, the GoGo Company website and its Sarkari Sarthi listings section. Our games and the full Sarkari Sarthi portal are separate products hosted on their own sites (GitHub Pages); they may have their own features, saves and privacy pages. For anything that happens on those product sites, check the privacy information published there - this policy does not make claims on their behalf.

What this website does not collect

  • We do not require accounts or sign-ups on gogocompany.in.
  • We do not ask for your name, phone number or email unless you contact us first.
  • We run no advertising networks and no third-party analytics or tracking scripts on this website.
  • The Sarkari listings page fetches only public job-data files in your browser; it sends us no personal data.

What may touch our systems

  • Web server logs: our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log standard technical request data such as IP address, browser type and requested pages, for security and abuse prevention. That data is handled under GitHub's own privacy policy.
  • Emails you send us: if you email us, we keep your message and address only to reply to you.
  • Browser storage: nothing is stored in your browser by this website.

Our products

Arrow Out! and Crystal Quest store game progress in your own browser's local storage on their own sites; we never receive that data. The full Sarkari Sarthi portal publishes public job listings and optional Telegram/WhatsApp notifications; nothing you do on gogocompany.in is shared with it. Each product's own site states what it stores.

External links

Product pages, game links and Sarkari listings link to third-party and government websites. Once you leave gogocompany.in, their privacy policies apply, not ours.

Children

Our website and games are family-friendly and do not require any personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page with a new effective date.
