Privacy Policy

Short version: we collect almost nothing. No accounts, no tracking pixels, no selling of data.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not require accounts or sign-ups on any of our products.
  • We do not ask for your name, phone number or email unless you contact us first.
  • We do not run third-party advertising or tracking networks on this website.
  • Our games save progress in your own browser's local storage - it stays on your device and we never receive it.

Information that may touch our systems

  • Web server logs: our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log standard technical request data such as IP address, browser type and requested pages, for security and abuse prevention.
  • Emails you send us: if you email us, we keep your message and address only to reply to you.
  • Sarkari Sarthi: the job listings page fetches publicly available data files from GitHub in your browser. No personal data is sent anywhere by the listing page.

External links

Our Sarkari listings link to official government websites and other third-party sites. Once you leave gogocompany.in, their privacy policies apply, not ours.

Children

Our games are family-friendly and do not collect any personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page with a new effective date.
