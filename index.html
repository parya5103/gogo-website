About Gogo Company

Gogo Company (gogocompany.in) is an independent Indian technology, digital media and web gaming organization headquartered in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India. Founded by Pratik Arya, Gogo Company develops browser games and operates digital information and media portals.

Its products and guides include Sarkari Sarthi (government recruitment and career information), PikaNews (publisher-attributed digital news), Arrow Out! (HTML5 logic puzzle game), Crystal Quest (interactive match-three arcade game), and the GTA 6 guide (an independently maintained reference with links to official Rockstar resources).
